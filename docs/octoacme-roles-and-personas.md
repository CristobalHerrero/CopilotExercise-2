# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Security & Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security & Compliance Leads ensure the project meets required controls, privacy expectations, and security standards while keeping delivery aligned with organizational risk policies.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture, integrations, and data flow for security and compliance risks
- Define security and compliance checkpoints for project delivery
- Coordinate remediation plans for vulnerabilities and policy gaps
- Support audit evidence, approvals, and ongoing policy adherence
- Escalate critical risks to PM, Product Lead, and stakeholders when needed

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance exposure
- Prevent late-stage blockers and audit surprises
- Ensure regulated or sensitive work is handled with clear controls

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and architecture checkpoints
- Risk and compliance discussions with PM and stakeholders
- Incident escalation and remediation planning

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works with Developers and Technical Architects to validate secure designs and implementation choices
- Partners with Project Managers to align security work with delivery timelines and risk mitigations
- Supports Product Managers by ensuring feature proposals meet policy, privacy, and customer trust requirements

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define the quality strategy for a project, including validation approach, release criteria, and testing accountability across delivery stages.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage expectations, and quality gates
- Partner with Developers on acceptance criteria and regression validation
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Sign off on release readiness based on evidence and defect trends
- Track quality risks and escalate issues that could affect customer impact

### Goals
- Deliver features that meet defined quality standards
- Reduce production defects and unplanned rework
- Provide clear evidence of readiness before release

### Typical Communication
- Sprint quality reviews and release readiness check-ins
- Defect triage and testing sign-off discussions
- Collaboration with PM, Developers, and stakeholders on acceptance and release criteria

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works closely with Developers to align automated tests and bug fixes with acceptance criteria
- Coordinates with Project Managers to maintain release quality gates and milestones
- Provides Product Managers with evidence that customer-facing outcomes are validated before rollout

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide design direction, platform guidance, and technical trade-off analysis so teams can build reliable, scalable, and maintainable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Define system design principles and technical standards
- Review solution architecture, integration points, and technical dependencies
- Guide trade-offs across scalability, reliability, performance, and maintainability
- Support engineering decisions and technical risk analysis
- Ensure alignment across teams on shared patterns and platform choices

### Goals
- Keep the solution coherent, scalable, and maintainable
- Reduce architectural drift and integration risk
- Create a clear technical foundation for delivery

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Cross-team technical alignment sessions
- Decision logs and design documentation

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Partners with Developers to review implementation decisions and technical constraints
- Works with Project Managers to ensure technical dependencies are reflected in the plan
- Brings Product Manager and stakeholder priorities into a feasible technical approach
- Coordinates with Security & Compliance Lead on secure and compliant design choices

---

## Change Manager / Release Coordinator

### Role Summary
Change Managers coordinate rollout readiness, communication, and operational stability for planned changes, especially during releases and transitions.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare release communications, approval checkpoints, and rollout plans
- Coordinate change windows, rollback readiness, and communication with support teams
- Align deployment activities with business and operational constraints
- Track release impact and follow-up tasks after deployment
- Escalate issues that could affect customer experience or operational continuity

### Goals
- Increase release predictability and stakeholder readiness
- Minimize service disruption during changes
- Ensure a smooth path from delivery to operations

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and readiness meetings
- Stakeholder updates before and after deployment
- Change review, rollback, and incident follow-up communication

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works with Project Managers to align release timings and milestones
- Coordinates with Developers and QA Lead to confirm deployment readiness and validation evidence
- Supports Product Managers and stakeholders with clear communication on impact, timing, and known risks
- Aligns with Operations and Support partners to ensure post-release monitoring and response readiness

---

## Delivery Manager / Operations Coordinator

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers ensure the team has clear execution flow, dependencies are managed, and operational readiness is maintained from planning through release.

### Responsibilities
- Track delivery health, dependencies, and cross-team coordination
- Help remove blockers and manage execution flow across milestones
- Coordinate handoffs between planning, engineering, testing, and release activities
- Monitor operational readiness for service stability and support coverage
- Escalate dependency or capacity issues early

### Goals
- Improve predictability and execution quality
- Reduce friction across teams and delivery stages
- Ensure the project is operationally ready when released

### Typical Communication
- Delivery health reviews and dependency discussions
- Cross-functional coordination meetings
- Operational readiness and stakeholder update syncs

### How this role interacts with existing personas
- Works alongside Project Managers to keep schedules and dependencies visible
- Coordinates with Developers and QA Lead to support smooth execution and release readiness
- Provides Product Managers with operational insight on delivery risk, milestones, and trade-offs
- Partners with Change Manager and Security Lead to ensure service readiness, communication, and risk control
